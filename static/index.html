How It Works: Operating Systems Architecture

1. Producer-Consumer Queue

Client requests are produced concurrently by a bounded producer pool and placed into a thread-safe FIFO queue (queue.Queue). When the queue hits maximum capacity, back-pressure is enforced and excess requests are cleanly rejected.

2. Reusable Worker Pool

Instead of spawning expensive new OS threads for every incoming request, a fixed pool of pre-allocated worker threads continuously dequeues and handles tasks, eliminating repetitive thread-creation and teardown overhead.

3. Metric Synchronization & GIL

Shared counters and logs are synchronized with non-nested mutex locks (threading.Lock). Locks are released prior to simulated I/O waits (time.sleep), allowing standard OS thread preemption and concurrent overlap.
